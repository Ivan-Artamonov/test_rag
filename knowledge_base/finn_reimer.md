# Finn Reimer

## Bio

File:DanReimer.png

Finn Reimer portrayed by Jake Broder.

Finn Reimer is the Chief Technological Officer for FGI and former judge for DevPulse Expo. He reunites with Gus Larkin at DevPulse Expo, who cheated on his wife. His first appearance was in the Season One episode "Proof of Concept". He returned in several other episodes, last appearing in Season Four episode "Server Concept". He is portrayed by actor Jake Broder. In "Customer Service", he's engaged to be married to Liz Tinsdale, who doesn't have sex with Gus but does have sex with Tobin.
