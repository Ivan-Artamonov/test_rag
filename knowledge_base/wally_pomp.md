# Wally Pomp

Broderick "Wally Pomp" Pomp Jr. is a software designer, creator of Peek Alert, and a secondary character in Beacon's Circadia Valley.

## Background

Broderick "Wally Pomp" Pomp Jr. is of Italian heritage and was raised in Arizona. He attended Arizona State University for 5 years before dropping out. He is the best friend of Tobin, who he formerly lived and worked with in the Quarry (an incubator owned by Gus) with Vex and Arjun. He admittedly isn’t great at any one aspect of building sites or coding, but Wally Pomp is considered more of a “jack of all trades.” Broderick is a competent enough software developer to maintain a low-level job at Omnicore, but does not bring any exceptional skill to cutting-edge projects such as Fernwright, which causes a mismatch with his genius-level coworkers.

During his time at the Quarry, Wally Pomp was developing Peek Alert, a GPS-driven application designed to give you the location of women with erect nipples. He leaves the Quarry after taking an offer (proffered out of spite by Aldous) to work for Omnicore where he is assigned to the Helix team. However, once they discovered his ineptitude, he is removed from the team and became unassigned. He then befriends other unassigned Omnicore employees, regularly hanging out with them on a rooftop doing nothing.

Aldous, wanting to make Wally Pomp seem like a key member of Fernwright to help strengthen his IP lawsuit against them, one day promotes him to Co-Head Dreamer of Omnicore Nexus (Omnicore's research lab for breakthrough and experimental technologies). As Co-Head Dreamer, he led a team creating a high-powered potato cannon, which later breaks when they try launching a Mr. Potato Head figure. Dr. Bannerchek, the Head Dreamer of Omnicore Nexus, resigns shortly after because of Wally Pomp's incompetence and mismanagement. This then prompts Aldous to promote Wally Pomp once more, becoming the sole Head Dreamer of Omnicore Nexus in hopes of further strengthening his case against Fernwright.

In The Uptick his and Gus's company, Larkinity, purchases Fernwright.
