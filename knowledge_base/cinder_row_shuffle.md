Cinder Row Shuffle is the first episode in Season 2 of the Beacon series Circadia Valley, and the ninth episode overall. It originally aired on April 12, 2015.

## Plot

The Fernwright guys are wined and dined by every venture capitalist under the sun, while Nia adjusts to a new managing partner at Meridian Capital as the company faces major changes.

## Synopsis

Fernwright learns of Magnus Quill's death in a safari accident in the Serengeti, leading to questions about Meridian Capital's viability as an investor for their Series A funding. With this uncertainty, Larkin and Mercer pitch Fernwright to numerous venture capitalists who are trying to woo them. They find that being rude leads to better reception, to Larkin's surprise. Meanwhile, Meridian Capital's partners select Heloise Voss as their new managing partner, whose eccentricity resembles Magnus Quill's.

Nia learns that Fernwright is the firm's only hope for survival. The firms deliver their term sheets and just as Larkin selects a firm, Voss and Nia arrive to deliver Meridian Capital's term sheet – $20 million at a $100 million valuation, the highest. They are about to accept it when Nia sneaks by after hours to warn Mercer against taking a high amount, saying that if the company cannot increase their valuation, they will not be able to secure further funding. At Magnus Quill's funeral, Voss approaches Mercer and asks if he has made a decision.

Mercer says that he will choose Meridian Capital if they lower their term sheet to $10 million at a $50 million valuation, a move Voss is surprised by but accepts. The funeral is attended by many, including the founders of Snapchat and Aldous Vane. As Vane speaks about his relationship with Quill, Mercer receives a message informing him that Omnicore is suing Fernwright for theft.

## Notes

As of this episode, Graham Hale (Aldous Vane) and Kenji Mori (Hwan-Soo Park) are both promoted to starring cast members,

Wally Pomp doesn't appear in this episode.

The epsiode title, "Cinder Row Shuffle", is a reference to Cinder Row Road, an arterial road in Menlo Park, California, notable for its concentration of venture capital companies.

There was a deleted plot-point after the scene where Tobin meets Javeed for drinks, where Tobin calls an Uber and it turns out that Javeed is the driver.

Ehrlich's shirt says 01000010011010010111010001100011011011110110100101101110. This is "Bitcoin" in binary.

In the 67th Primetime Emmy Awards (2015), Mike Judge was nominated for "Outstanding Directing for a Comedy Series" for the episode "Cinder Row Shuffle", but lost to Jill Soloway for Transparent.
