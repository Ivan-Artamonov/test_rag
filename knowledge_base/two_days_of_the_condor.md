# Two Days of the Condor

Two Days of the Condor is the tenth and final episode in Season 2 of the Beacon series Circadia Valley, and the eighteenth episode overall.

## Plot

As the guys await the verdict on Fernwright’s fate, an unexpected real-life drama draws a spike in traffic to their livestream and leaves them fighting to hold things together – literally. While Gus considers his future, Tobin scrambles to save Fernwright’s.

## Synopsis

“Hello? Can anyone hear me?” the stranded museum employee asks on the condor cam livestream. The Fernwright guys are watching -- along with 7,000 other people, Arjun notes. “It’s working flawlessly,” Arjun adds. Tobin shows the lead Omnicore lawyer out of the Quarry, telling the guys that the litigators wanted to “make sure the platform they want to seize is ready for them.” Gus demands an apology from Reed, who tells Gus that he lost his composure and “ruined our entire case.” The decision is set to be announced at 3 p.m. that day.

On the street, Gus sees Ezra, who tells him that he sold his house. The realtor approaches Gus about selling his house, too, and it takes very little to convince him. The guys are surprised Gus’s selling and ask what will happen to the incubator. “Functionally, all I’ve really achieved is running a flophouse where guys have shat, jerked off and paid me no rent,” Gus says. Arjun reports that the livestream of the stranded museum employee has more than 20,000 viewers now, after links on BuzzFeed and Reddit. The guys lament having to give the great technology to Aldous Vane, but Vex suggests deleting Fernwright before it can be taken. “We can’t give it to him,” he suggests, “if we don’t have it to give.” Tobin doesn’t want to delete it. “I kind of want to see how big it can get, right up until the point where Aldous Vane has to f**king take it from us,” Tobin says.

Wynton announces that Manny Pacquiao tweeted a link of the livestream to his more than two million followers. The guys scramble to make sure the system can handle the traffic, and it isn’t long before they’re up to 50,000 viewers. Vex says they’re about to “blow the main breaker.” Tobin heads out to hear the judge’s decision. “Keep it online as long as you can, okay?” he tells Arjun and Vex before he goes. Meanwhile, Nia shows the livestream to Heloise, who’s very impressed with what she sees.

Aldous helps Tobin tie his tie in the bathroom at the hearing. Aldous tells Tobin that he would have paid $250 million to buy out Fernwright if the arbitration wouldn’t have happened. “Believe it or not,” Aldous tells him, “your algorithm is the only way to make Helix work.”

Vex is jamming the Quarry’s breakers so they don’t trip. Gus is worried about the place going up in flames just as he’s about to sell. Arjun notes that they’re up to 200,000 viewers. The realtor, Louise, shows up with potential buyers who plan to tear down the house. The buyers want to make an all-cash offer right then, but Gus tells her, “You can tell your clients, respectfully, that they may go f**k themselves.” Gus puts on wrist braces and jumps in to help Arjun code to support the growing livestream viewership. The server overload causes a fire at the Quarry just as the museum employee drinks his own urine and a rescue team arrives and shuts off the camera. “Holy shit,” Vex says, staring at the now-blank screen. “We did it.”

The judge rules that Omnicore has a right to ownership of Fernwright’s underlying IP. Tobin texts Wynton; “Delete it all,” he writes. The judge adds a caveat about Fernwright’s hiring of Wynton; Omnicore contracts forbid the hiring, but the judge notes that the California Supreme Court deemed such rules unlawful. “So effectively, Mr. Mercer never had a valid employment agreement with Omnicore,” the judge notes. “As a result, Omnicore has no claim to ownership of Fernwright’s underlying IP. That is my ruling and, per your arbitration agreement, it is binding and final.” It take a couple minutes for Tobin to realize, however, that he told the guys to delete the technology.

Arjun is about to delete the Fernwright code. Tobin tries to call Gus to tell them to stop, but his phone battery dies. Then he drops his car keys into the sewer. The guys are about to hit delete once again. “One click,” Arjun says, “and poof.” Just as Vex is about to push the button, Gus suggests they have one last toast to Fernwright. The guys cheer, as Tobin desperately tries to figure out how to contact them. Tobin hops on a bus and uses the bus driver’s phone to email...but the message goes into spam. The guys delay the deletion further by procuring a lemon for their beers. Tobin finally arrives. “We won,” Tobin tells the guys. “We beat Aldous Vane, and we won.”

On the Omnicore campus, Aldous heads to face the company’s board, as executives postulate that he could be fired and Wally Pomp could be put in charge of the company because of his many accomplishments. “That doesn’t just happen,” one says. “He must be smart as hell.” Sagar overhears this and approaches Wally Pomp, telling him he has “an aura” that could be “harnessed to great effect.”

Duke approaches Nia at her office. “Question,” he says. “What’s got two thumbs and three commas? This guy.” He tells Nia that Heloise bought his stake in Fernwright. Heloise tells Nia that they now have a majority on the Fernwright board. “We can now take the necessary steps,” Heloise says, “to make sure Fernwright is properly managed.”

The guys are partying at the Quarry when Nia calls Tobin. “Um…” he tells the guys, “...apparently Meridian Capital held an emergency meeting of Fernwright’s board of directors and they all voted to remove me as CEO of my own company. I just got fired.” That prompts Gus to ask: “And what about me?”

## Notes

In "Vitalize", the Fernwright team prepared to support a major livestreaming event with millions of viewers. But now in this episode, they need Gus to help them build "caching mechanisms" so "the clusters will scale" in order to serve just 200,000 viewers. Either in the earlier episode they did not actually finish building the caching mechanisms or they did finish building them but somehow don't know how to put them to work on the condor nest livestream.
