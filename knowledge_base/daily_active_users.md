# Daily Active Users

Daily Active Users is the ninth episode of Season 3, and the twenty-seventh episode overall. It originally aired on June 19, 2016.

## Synopsis

Shocking stats are revealed and prompt Tobin to bridge the gap between Fernwright and its users, but Wynton must go to extremes to keep everything intact. Aldous tries to recapture his former glory by bringing in new talent after discovering secrets about the competition.

## Plot

Fernwright debuts a new high-quality advertisement featuring “tables” as a metaphor for the platform, and Gus and Heloise Voss make awkward small talk at a party at her house to celebrate 500,000 installs of Fernwright.

At the party, Tobin tells Nia that while the app has a lot of installs, the number of daily active users is appallingly low, proving that Nia’s concern should not have been ignored. Arjun, Gus, and Vex enjoy the celebration, but Wynton, who is aware of the numbers, nervously flits around.

Nia sets up a focus group to get feedback for the platform and the focus group reveals that everyone was “totally freaked out.” Tobin and Nia realize since they never gave the beta to “regular people,” they never got feedback on what “regular people” thought of it until now. Tobin, who can’t stand the negative feedback, storms into the room to explain the intricacies of the platform. In full-on professor mode, Tobin spends his afternoon with the participants until each person understands the function of the platform.

Tobin returns to the Quarry and explains to the guys why the platform is tanking. When Arjun suggests changing the platform, Tobin replies, “If you build an airplane, and people are afraid of anything that flies, you can’t just take the wings off of it, because at that point, all you’re left with is a really slow, super-expensive, shitty bus.” Tobin proposes spending the rest of the funding on educating the public about how to use Fernwright. Gus attempts to rally the group to support Tobin, even if his plan is a suicide mission. All of the newly hired employees quit.

Recently banished, Aldous has lunch with Sagar and tries to figure out his next move. Otis, Omnicore’s Head of Security, alerts him that a former Fernwright employee is interviewing on the Omnicore campus. Aldous now has a shot at redeeming himself with the Omnicore board.

After meeting with the employee, Aldous brings his discovery about Fernwright’s daily active users to the disinterested Omnicore board. He pretends that he lied about wanting to create a platform, and that his efforts were a charade because he knew any compression platform was destined to fail. The board is skeptical, but they give him another chance when he brings in Declan Finch, the new Head of Omnicore-Cubix Product, to reveal what Vane has supposedly in fact been working toward: The Cube.

As Tobin continues his quest to explain the platform, Fernwright’s money dwindles and the daily active user number stagnates. Tobin returns to the ad agency, where he asks them to create something to clarify the platform for little money. They show him “Pipey” an animated character that lives on the site and troubleshoots user issues. Tobin accepts defeat, “Face it, Wynton. Being too early is the same as being wrong.”

After he closes the deal with Cobalt Analytics to store “The Cube,” Aldous’s full status as CEO is restored. He leads the board out to the Omnicore courtyard where they find an elephant, because an elephant “never forgets,” and neither does Aldous. As Tobin prepares to dissolve the company, the guys learn that the number of daily active users have gone up. While they celebrate, Wynton takes a call and orders more users from a click farm in Bangladesh.
