# Optimal Tip-to-Tip Efficiency

" Last night I was watching my friends here have this argument. About, you know, manipulating data And, you know, how many datas could one guy manipulate at once and, uh And I was just I was thinking. Maybe it could be another way, you know? Something that I would call, "middle out"."

―Tobin during Fernwright's presentation

Optimal Tip-to-Tip Efficiency is the eighth and last episode in season 1 of the Beacon series Circadia Valley. It originally aired on June 1, 2014.

## Plot

Poised to compete at DevPulse Expo, the guys of Fernwright become worried after an impressive presentation by Aldous Vane. As Wynton tries to pivot the company, Tobin is inspired to make big changes at the last minute.

## Synopsis

Desperate to avoid litigation after the brawl, the head of the Expo apologizes to the Fernwright gang for the attack by Finn Reimer while they were presenting. The guy says that Finn Reimer will never be a judge again and that he was fired from Oracle and his wife left him. To dissuade the guys from taking legal action against DevPulse, the guy says he’ll send Fernwright straight through to the finals, where they will compete for the grand prize. Gus gets the boys’ hotel accommodations upgraded as well.

Back in the presentation hall, Aldous Vane is presenting Helix. Tobin assures Arjun that Helix can’t come close to Fernwright’s Kraus index, a measure of how well files compress. Aldous Vane reveals that Helix’ Kraus index is the best in the history of compression: 2.89, which happens to be the same as Fernwright’s. “Anyone who tells you that their platform is faster than ours better have good lawyers,” Aldous Vane says. His presentation ends with a performance by Shakira.

Tobin is depressed because Omnicore reverse-engineered Fernwright’s compression algorithm. “At best, we’re a worse version of Helix,” he tells the guys. Gus says they’re fine and heads off to “get inside” Aldous Vane’s head, while Wynton suggests all they have to do is pivot Fernwright into a product that people want. Wynton heads out to poll people on the street about what Fernwright should be, a creepy situation that eventually finds him apprehended by cops.

While walking around among the booths at DevPulse, Vex and Arjun see the company Kwerpy. “That could have been us,” Arjun says of Kwerpy. The pair talk about how it would be a “dickhead move” to leave Tobin before Fernwright even goes under. Just as Arjun and Vex are about to ask the Kwerpy founder if he’d hire them, the Kwerpy founder asks them to hire him at Fernwright -- Kwerpy is about to go under itself.

Tobin runs into Nia as she heads out of the conference -- Magnus Quill called her back to Palo Alto after seeing the presentations by Gus and Aldous Vane. Nia says that she’d like to get a drink with Tobin, especially now that they won’t be working together anymore.

Tobin is fretting about the Fernwright presentation, but Gus is still very positive about the whole thing. While listening to Vex, Arjun, and Gus talk about a silly formula for jerking off an entire room of guys, Tobin is struck by an epiphany about Fernwright, which inspires him to rework his product. Tobin programs through the night and says the changes are significant enough that he should present. Nia is about to text Tobin a good luck message, but then she decides to head to the presentation.

Tobin is nervous and seems like he’s going to blow the presentation, but then he starts to explain Fernwright, saying that they used to be cloud-based, user-focused compression. He continues by saying that Omnicore did everything they were trying to do, so he changed what Fernwright is. He rebuilt the algorithm and tells the audience he got a Kraus index of 3.8, far beyond what was formerly the theoretical limit of 2.89, which Omnicore achieved. The crowd gasps, and one of the judges asks him to repeat that stat. The judge gives Tobin a file to compress without any degradation: a 132 gigabyte uncompressed 3D video. The file compresses, but Tobin is sure that something is wrong as the file is only about half the size it should be. He runs a Kraus test, and the score is far better than he expected: 5.2, double the best Kraus index ever. Fernwright wins the competition’s cup and the $50,000 prize.

After the win, Nia shows up to congratulate Tobin. Overwhelmed, Tobin runs out the back door and, yet again, barfs in a Dumpster.

## Notes

This episode was dedicated to Arthur Vale, who played Magnus Quill in the first five episodes of Season 1.

At the 66th Primetime Emmy Awards, Marcus Hale was nominated for Outstanding Writing in a Comedy Series for his work on this episode, but lost to Louis C.K. for Louie.

The concept of "Optimal Tip-to-Tip Efficiency" was actually written up as a 12-page paper that became the foundation for the dialogue the guys deliver during the hotel room scene. Following the episode, the paper was put online to prove its plausibility.

The Kraus Index is a fictional performance score for lossless data compression. It was invented by Amos Kraus and Vikram Rao at Kernbridge University specifically for use in the Circadia Valley series.
