Eli Cross served as lawyer for Magnus Quill before being served as outside counsel for Fernwright. His first appearance was in the Season One episode "Fiduciary Duties". He is portrayed by actor Ben Feldman.

## Character history

In 2014, Eli Cross served as the attorney for Meridian Capital under the management of Magnus Quill, but left to be the consolation manager for Fernwright.
