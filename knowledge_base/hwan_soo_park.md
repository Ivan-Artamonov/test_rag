# Hwan-Soo Park

Hwan-Soo Park is a shady Chinese app developer living in Gus Larkin's The Quarry and business incubator, Skylark. Gus taken a special interest in him, despite the pair’s occasional problems communicating. He is a recurring character. He is the only incubator resident who isn't involved with Fernwright.

## Background

He was a resident in Gus's incubator, He now lives in the same house Gus Larkin lived at in Tibet, China using Gus's identity . He is a Mandarin-speaking Chinese male seen to be in his late teens to mid-twenties. He has a quiet demeanor and has difficulty understanding and speaking the English language. This aggravated Gus when he explains house rules to Hwan-Soo Park such as not burning household trash, how to recycle, and the need to clear fish carcasses from the kitchen sink. He was often was seen opening the front door to Gus's house for guests to come in or irritating Gus for comedic effect.

During his stay at Gus's The Quarry, Hwan-Soo Park developed an app that is able to locate the least-crowded playgrounds so parents can bring their kids to these desirable locations. However, it was flawed, since pedophiles could also use the app to prey on children. Gus suggested and had Hwan-Soo Park pivot the app to locate nearby smokers instead and named it "PuffTrack", a play on the words "Smoker" and "Location". PuffTrack was met favourably at Meridian Capital after it was pitched to Heloise Voss. Unfortunately, Hwan-Soo Park was found smoking within the Meridian Capital premises after his pitch, since his app was soon to receive seed funding and he only smokes for celebratory purposes.

Not much is known of Hwan-Soo Park's background, or how he came into meeting Gus Larkin. During his time living with Larkin, he served primarily as a personal assistant until Gus no longer required his services and tried to evict him. Inadvertently, Gus revealed California's squatters rights, barring him from eviction and granting him free housing for up to a year.

## Relationships

### Gus Larkin

Though initially an employe of Larkin, primarily as a household assistant, Hwan-Soo Park's relationship progressively devolved after his attempted eviction. Hwan-Soo Park now regularly attempts to sabotage Larkin in attempts to prank phone call him for unknown reasons other than to fool Larkin.

### Unknown Guests

Hwan-Soo Park seems to have, or at one point had, a variety of Asian-American guests who periodically sleep over at Larkin's estate. They thus far have only made one appearance and none have been named as of yet.
