# Priya

## Bio

File:Priya2.jpg

Priya portrayed by Bridey Elliott

Priya is an engineer for Facebook, and was previously the love interest for Tobin Mercer but left him due to his position on spaces vs. tabs. Her first appearance was in the Season Three episode "Larkinity Insanity". She is portrayed by actor Bridey Elliott.
