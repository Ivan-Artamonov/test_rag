# Mateo Cruz

## Bio

File:MateoCruz.png

Mateo Cruz portrayed by Dante Reyes.

Mateo Cruz is a street artist who caught the attention of Gus Larkin. He was hired to do the logo for Fernwright, but his logo was scrapped for being too vulgar, although his artwork was sold to Aldous Vane for $500,000. His only appearance was in the Season One episode "Signaling Risk". He is portrayed by actor Dante Reyes.
