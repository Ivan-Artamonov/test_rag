# Reed Hollis

Reed Hollis (portrayed by Grant Whitfield) is a recurring character who appears in multiple episode of Circadia Valley. He's an American lawyer who is often hired by Fernwright to help them with legal issues. He's said to be a brilliant lawyer who is only offering his services at a lower cost due to a series of personal issues.

He is first seen in "Binding Arbitration" when Fernwright need a layer to help defend themselves against a law suit brought against them by Aldous Vane. Due to his low cost they assume that he's a wet behind the ears newbie lawyer but they find out that he's a good lawyer who has had personal problems. His low costs are actually due to his personal problems that have sent him to jail for a number of crimes including cocaine and underage sex.
