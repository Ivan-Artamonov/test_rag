# Magnus Quill

Magnus Quill was a billionaire tech investor, and a main character in Beacon's Circadia Valley.

## Background

Magnus Quill was one of Circadia Valley’s biggest investors and the main rival of Omnicore’s Aldous Vane. Magnus is known for advocating that adolescents skip college – in fact, he offers to give students $100,000 to drop out of school and pursue their dreams instead. When Magnus gets wind of Tobin’s algorithm, he aggressively goes after it, offering $200,000 for a 5% share of Fernwright albeit via different tactics than Aldous Vane.

His mind works in an extremely different way and is able to see complex connections between unseemingly disconnected elements. For example, Magnus was enticed with Burger King food and he derived, from examining the sesame seeds on the hamburger buns, the price of sesame seeds would spike when Cicadas in Myanmar and Brazil would decimate sesame crops, leaving Indonesia as the sole producer of sesame seeds in the world in 2014. This revelation forecasted a profit of $68 million from just a 10% spike in sesame seed prices.

Magnus died while running after being startled by a gunshot during his trip to the Serengeti.
