# Founder Friendly

Founder Friendly is the first episode of the third season of the Beacon series Circadia Valley, and the nineteenth episode overall. It originally aired on April 24, 2016.

## Plot

After being unceremoniously fired, an angry Tobin faces a tough decision: whether to accept the diminished role of CTO, or leave Fernwright behind for good. Gus takes a shine to Heloise's new choice of CEO, Declan Finch, while Arjun and Vex weigh their options in Tobin's absence. At Omnicore, Aldous tries to improve his image by admitting failure, and Wally Pomp gets wind of some major changes.

## Synopsis

After learning he’s been fired from Fernwright, Tobin heads to Meridian Capital to confront Heloise and the board. Heloise tells Tobin they voted to fund Fernwright at $5 million and to give it a valuation of $50 million. “You have literally created a company too valuable for you to run,” she explains, adding that they’re bringing in a new CEO. Refusing her offers to help pick the new CEO and to stay on as CTO, Tobin says he’ll sue. Eli informs him he’s no longer Tobin’s legal counsel, as he’s technically Fernwright’s corporate counsel, and he’s not about to sue himself.

The following day, Nia tries to explain why she voted against Tobin: If she hadn’t, Heloise would have removed her and still voted him out. “At least this way I’m still in the game,” she says. Adding insult to injury, Meridian Capital has already hired a new CEO for Fernwright: Declan Finch, the head of Entercross Systems, which IPO’d in 1998 for more than a billion dollars.

After Wynton pledges his allegiance to Tobin, Arjun and Vex acknowledge to each other they want to stay at Fernwright. At Omnicore, Aldous Vane announces they’re getting rid of Helix and terminating all its employees.

Arjun and Vex discuss Tobin: He’s a brilliant coder, but lacks managerial skills. Instead of prefacing each complaint with a positive note on Tobin’s character, they decide to use the acronym RIGBY: Tobin Is Great, But Y’know… They agree Tobin is arrogant to assume they’d walk out on a payday after giving up their own apps to work on his.

Wynton presents Tobin with a stack of companies offering to hire him as CTO. “You are the belle of the ball,” he explains, “and these are all your swains, hoping for a glimpse of ankle.”

Gus meets with Declan Finch at Meridian Capital, armed with the best Friars’ Roast old man jokes, and is completely charmed by him. Apparently, he’s a longtime fan of Gus’s work and Skylark.

Tobin reviews the companies in contention and decides Flutterbeam is the best fit. Gus tries to talk Tobin into meeting with Declan Finch first, believing a partnership between him and Tobin would make Fernwright a “decacorn”: the rare company to have a $10 billion valuation before its IPO. Arjun and Vex take the opportunity to tell Tobin they’re staying on at Fernwright, and that they believe they can scale the app without him. He storms out to meet with Flutterbeam immediately.

At Flutterbeam, the founders tell Tobin they want him on a secret project -- an app that puts mustaches on your face during live chats. Despite being underwhelmed by the idea, Tobin calls his lawyer, Reed Hollis, to review the contract, but learns he’s in prison following a violent bender sparked by the alcohol in kombucha tea. Tobin meets with Hollis at the prison, who tells him to swallow his pride and take the CTO job at Fernwright.

Arjun and Vex quickly discover they can’t scale without Tobin after all, and hope they can still quit on the grounds of their loyalty to Tobin.

Wally Pomp signs an Omnicore termination contract, agreeing not to disparage Aldous or the company publicly or privately, earning $20 million for his silence.

Tobin meets with Declan, who accepts Tobin’s decision not to work at Fernwright. Declan says he won’t take the CEO job without him, and kindly shows Tobin out, wishing him well in his future endeavors. Tobin is barely out of the driveway before he stops the car and realizes what he’s giving up. He puts his car in reverse and heads back.
