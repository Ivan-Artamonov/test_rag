# Exit Event

Summary: The Fernwright team attempts to sabotage the company's million-dollar launch in order to prevent the decentralized system from potentially abolishing internet privacy.

## Plot

So, I did the right thing, I helped save the world, my legs are still super sore, and my reward is that I'm poor.

— Arjun

As 10 years passes since the events of 'Exit Event', the Fernwright team reflect on what they had been a part of. A flashback is shown from 10 years earlier, where the Fernwright team is celebrating closing a $100 million deal with AT&T to build their decentralized Internet on their platform. Arjun is singing (How To Be) A Millionaire, but is interrupted by Wynton, who brings out a cake with Tobin in it. Duke Remington reveals he purchased the shares from Wally Pomp's father.

Tobin talks about his experience at Fernwright

Nia hands Tobin a gift, a framed screenshot of their text conversation as a gift. However, the screenshot leaves Tobin confused, as the text he sent contained 4 dots after the "is", not 3. Nia informs Hwan-Soo Park that Gus's Fernwright Coin is now worth $20 million, and Hwan-Soo Park sets out to steal Gus's identity.

Leaving AT&T. Just dotted the I's, crossed the T's, and every single detail is....perfect.

Tobin calls in Arjun, Nia, and Wynton, showing the text and how Tobin sent 4 dots to Nia, while Nia received 3 dots. He concludes that his system is compressing the content of the encrypted messages in transit, bypassing the encryption. Arjun responds that this is impossible, confusing Tobin. Vex comes into the room bringing Clif bars and a gun, telling Wynton to bolt the doors. Vex explains that, while he initially dismissed what Tobin said, he realizes that the network's AI compression cracked the encryption in order to be more efficient, potentially bringing the end of global privacy and the beginning of the apocalypse. Vex suggests shutting it down to negative feedback.

Tobin tries to figure out the reason why his text was different on her screen.

Tobin, after talking with Wynton on the roof, discusses with the rest of the team on how to fail the launch. Arjun disagrees with the rest of the team, telling the team that they are throwing away Tobin's work so that he can't get any money. However, he is interrupted when his Tesla drives off of the Fernwright parking lot and to a car wash on El Camino. Vex explains that he tasked the AI with getting Arjun's TLS key, which it gave within 2 hours. Arjun explains he cannot be a part of this, asking the team to revoke his permissions, delete his PiperMail account, and to not trust him.

The FernNet billboard.

Arjun, yearning for his level of fame, put on his old Fernwright outfit and went around to people in line waiting for the FernNet official launch. Meanwhile, Tobin and Vex discuss the plan and Arjun confirms he is not on the list for the FernNet launch. John notices a strange build being uploaded to the Fernwright supernode, and tells Gabe, who informs Arjun of the strange build and hands him an orange USB containing the build. Arjun runs to the supernode, sending a message to Wynton and Nia using voice, but the message received is different than what was sent. Vex gets a notification Arjun is trying to log into the supernode, and sends him a call. However, Arjun is too out of breath to explain anything.

Arjun having a selfie with a fan in line.

Vex calls Tobin, and merges Wynton and Nia. Arjun explains that they reverted the build from the supernode, and he needs access to deploy in order to fix the build. After receiving no response from Tobin, Vex gives Arjun access and the build is restored. The build, while not generating enough noise to take down networks and power grids, caused the noise in the speakers to resonate, and unleashed rats onto the theater floor. Tobin closed Fernwright and liquidated its remaining assets. After some news clips and interviews, the flashback continues where the team is on the roof watching the Fernwright billboard being destroyed.

FernNet is live!

Tobin reveals he now teaches at Kernbridge as the "Aldous Vane Professor of Ethics in Technology" and that during his hiatus, he traveled to Tibet in order to find Gus, but to no avail. Wally Pomp also reveals he is the president of Kernbridge University. In a similar vein, Arjun and Vex are co-founders of Fenn Lane Strategic Systems, one of the largest cybersecurity firms. Nia works for a non-profit "think tank", Wynton works with the elderly, and Hwan-Soo Park took on the identity of Gus Larkin in order to claim his $20 million. During this time, someone had also purchased the Quarry from Hwan-Soo Park's estate. They sit around the old dining table where they used to work to play a game of "always green" together. The episode ends with Tobin attempting to show the documentary crew the hard drive containing the Fernwright compression algorithm but fails to find it as the credits roll.

Wally Pomp gives a speech at Kernbridge.

## Trivia

The last place the orange USB hard drive is seen is plugged into computer equipment at the very top of the Salesforce Tower.

An extended version of the mockumentary was released on YouTube a week after the episode initially aired entitled, Ten Years Later: The Extended Fernwright Documentary.

In Minimum Viable Product, Magnus Quill states after his TED Talk that Tobin should not go back to college. Ironically, Tobin would be a professor at Kernbridge.
