# Dorian Vex

Dorian Vex (refered simply as Vex by most characters) is one of the main characters of Circadia Valley, appearing in every season of the show. Portayed by Pavel Stein, Vex is a Canadian man living in the Circadia Valley area of Northern California, staying at Gus Larkin's The Quarry and working for Fernwright, which he was one of the founding members.

## Personality

Like Arjun, Vex is quick-witted and merciless, but in contrast, highly apathetic, sardonic and brutally honest. He often bickers with Arjun and competes with him in everything, and often wins, much to the chagrin of Arjun. He is a talented and confident self-taught coder, and enjoys manipulating Arjun by preying on his insecurities. He frequently makes jokes on Arjun’s ethnicity, not because he is racist but because it gets a rise out of Arjun. He has a penchant for hurting people deeply through his savage insults and unsettling stares. He is stoic and claims to be an anarcho-capitalist but mostly likely, pays taxes. Truth be told, Vex and Arjun often argue and bicker because of how similar they are in terms of talent and ability but and Vex only wins so much Arjun because just less self-assured and slightly less talented. Vex and Arjun are subconsciously each other’s best friends. He is a LaVeyan Satanist and likes heavy metal.

## Character History

### Pre-show

It’s not much known about Vex’s history before the first episode of the Season One. He was born in Toronto, Canada and at some point illegally entered and started living the United States some time before the start of the show.
In Season Two, Duke Remington comes to the house to meet the team for the first and when he reaches Vex, he says “I wanna know what kinda fucked up childhood this guy had” which causes Vex to stare at him and walk away instead of his usual biting comebacks. There are other hints that he doesn’t get on with his family like in Fiduciary Duties when he calls his Mom a “backstabbing bitch”.

### Circadia Valley

By the time of the first episode he is living in the Circadia Valley area of Northern California, staying at Gus Larkin's The Quarry where the other main characters live. When Tobin rejects the cash offers for his compression algorithm, He forms a company named Fernwright and hires Vex on to the team as the companies network engineer and system architect. Vex credits himself as an online security expert, and as such is responsible for system administration and server configuration at Fernwright.

He is arguably the most talented of the main cast and is very confident in his abilities and has proven his worth to the Fernwright team on multiple occasions, such as building the server farm "Anton", to save the company the struggle of paying for generic, mediocre servers in an office space. Unlike the other members of the cast, he has been shown to be skilled in both Hardware and Software side of IT, working on both through out the show.

Due to this, Vex takes great pride in his security tech, becoming very nervous and confused whenever his tech is compromised. He is shown to have a great reputation in the Circadia Valley as when he leaves Fernwright in Season three, he is almost immediately swarmed with requests from recruiters after he switches his LinkedIn status. They are still sending him gifts to entice him even when he is turning down their meetings.

In season 5, Vex is promoted to senior management along with Arjun with the title of "Chief Systems Architect". By Season 6, Vex is promoted to VP of Architecture at Fernwright but refuses to take anyone under his management, preferring to work on the Systems Architecture by himself. This leads to continual conflict with Head of HR Tracy Robertson. After Fernwright was shut down, Vex and Arjun began to run a large cybersecurity firm together.

## Relationships

### Arjun

See also: Arjun and Vex
Vex often plays mocking pranks on Arjun, but the two do appear to be friends or at most, frenemies despite this, with the two often bonding over their shared moral ambiguity. The two spend a lot of the series competing againts each other in such things like who is able to code faster or complete tasks faster. In the Series Finale, when Arjun informs the Fernwright that their plan to sabotage their own launch is compromised, he insists they trust him to upload the correct code into their network, despite telling them earlier not to trust him. When Tobin fails to decide if he can trust Arjun or not, Vex takes the initiative and grants Arjun access back into the network.

### Wynton Corbell

Vex also respects Wynton for his business development skills, business sense and savviness, dependability, and reliability. He describes him as"sharp" and once said "[Wynton] can do things in this house nobody else can". This was proven correct when it was seen how Gus was jealous of Wynton's skills.

### Tobin Mercer

While he is usually indifferent to Tobin and lacks loyalty towards him, Vex highly respects Tobin as a coder and admits that Fernwright is nothing without Tobin.

### Others

Vex has a girlfriend Saskia, who is also a LaVeyan Satanist, with the two having an open and long-standing relationship, although Saskia still lies to Vex about sleeping with other men.
In later seasons of the show he is shown to have struck up a friendship with Nia Calloway.

## Trivia

It is unknown how old Vex is, but it is assumed that he is in his 30s.

Vex is one of the only main characters to not have a Twitter account.
