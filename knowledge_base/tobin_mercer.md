# Tobin Mercer

## Bio

I always knew I was missing something, and then when someone explained the concept of ‘game’ I remember very distinctly thinking ‘That’s what I don’t have.

— Tobin Mercer, "Fiduciary Duties".

Tobin Mercer (played by Rowan Ashford) was the CEO and founder of Fernwright as well as FernNet, and is currently an Aldous Vane Professor of Ethics in Technology at Kernbridge. Before founding Fernwright, Tobin was an employee of Omnicore. At work he was often an antagonist to the codelads. While at Omnicore, Tobin worked on the Fernwright algorithm. However, even though Tobin created one line of code on the algorithm on an Omnicore laptop which would mean that Fernwright would be property of Omnicore, Tobin's contract with Omnicore was void due to a clause preventing him from leaving. Tobin continued to work on Fernwright until its demise, but still was involved with Fernwright projects such as FernChat and FernNet until the company would be shut down in 2019.

## Personality

Though timid, nervous, deeply insecure and inexcusably naive. Tobin is an extremely intelligent coder and very passionate about what he does. He can be a perfectionist and spends a lot of time making sure everything is fitting his vision, and has even refused to hire coders and date someone because of the way they wrote their code. His anxiety causes him to struggle with being a respectable figure to his friends, coworkers, and others he's in business with, and often becomes the target for bullying. However, as time goes on, he gains more confidence and becomes more abrasive and demanding.

### Season One

“

I'd like for this company to be different than Omnicore and Zorp and all the rest, you know? Like, let's not turn this into a corporate cult with bike meetings and voluntary retreats that are actually mandatory and claiming for the world to be a better place all the time.

”

Year synopsis

After founding Fernwright, Tobin left Omnicore to develop the company and its algorithm. At DevPulse Expo, he quickly realizes that Omnicore's Helix project is going as planned, and will be better than Fernwright. In a bathroom, Tobin comes up with fold-in compression and wins DevPulse Expo.

2014

"Minimum Viable Product": Tobin founds Fernwright and is stuck in a bidding war between rivals Aldous Vane and Magnus Quill.

"The Cap Table": Tobin hires Wynton Corbell to help develop a business plan for Magnus Quill.

"Articles of Incorporation": Tobin must negotiate rights for the Fernwright name.

"Fiduciary Duties": A drunk Tobin gives Gus Larkin a seat on the board and must figure out how to get him off.

"Signaling Risk": Tobin keeps developing Fernwright.

"Third Party Insourcing": Tobin hires a prodigy programmer known as The Chisel, but discovers that he destroyed Bank of America's systems on accident.

"Proof of Concept": Tobin gets distracted by an ex-girlfriend at DevPulse Expo.

"Optimal Tip-to-Tip Efficiency": Tobin comes up with fold-in compression in a bathroom and wins DevPulse Expo along with $50,000.
