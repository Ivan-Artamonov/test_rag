# Aldous Vane

Aldous Vane is a billionaire senior executive at Omnicore, and the main antagonist in Beacon's Circadia Valley.

## Background

Aldous Vane was the Chief Innovation Officer of Omnicore, a huge tech company in Circadia Valley that seemingly has its hands in everything but specifically does “targeted search and cross-platform verticals.” He has a long-standing rivalry with Magnus Quill, which only fuels his desire to acquire Tobin’s algorithm. For Aldous, bigger is better – and money is no object.

## Personality

Different traits of tech industry tycoons, but also state leaders, have been combined in Aldous Vane: he is a ruthless, egotistical, abusive, hot-tempered and sadistic megalomaniac, who makes grand statements and illustrates points with the help of endangered animals he presents in vivam on board meetings. Despite his duplicitous demonstration of humanism, citing values and wisdom of historical figures favored by the Circadia Valley, he shows little regard to his underlings, for example by not remembering their names or their faces. He is vindictive at all cost, even wasteful, and to a level where his vindictiveness is even triggered in minor disputes. The leadership culture he established leads him surrounded by nothing other than siccophants who are eager to do his bidding and hinge on every word he's saying, but also keep the truth from him when problems occur, leaving him to the embarassment by the public when he presents unfinished, mediocre products.

The culture of the Circadia Valley finds ways to reinforce his cruel nature, in exceptional siccophants who would do everything to please him. When Aldous reflected on his legacy and his treatment of people, his spiritual guide Sagar and his security chief Otis would collaborate to manipulate him back into the position of the angry and abusive leader he was.
