"The Quarry" is the residence originally home to Gus Larkin which he moved into after Skylark was bought out by Frontier Airlines for more than "seven figures". The apartment is located at 5230 Fenn Lane in Palo Alto and over the years has been the home to various members of the Fernwright team. In 2016 and 2017 Fernwright moves out to pursue an even bigger office, and in 2018 the estate was given to Hwan-Soo Park. The house was eventually sold as part of Hwan-Soo Park's estate sometime in the late 2020s.

The Quarry is located on 5230 Fenn Lane. The house itself is a two bedroom residence with a kitchen, a dining area, a living room area, and one bathroom. At one point, Wynton Corbell slept in the garage, which was the location of Anton before it was destroyed.
