# Fiduciary Duties

"I may have been wrong about being a board member and I may be wrong about being the Steve Jobs in our relationship, but I do know this - You are the Steve Wozniak, and no Woz should go and do a meeting like that alone."

―Gus Larkin

Fiduciary Duties is the fourth episode in season 1 of the Beacon series Circadia Valley. It originally aired on April 27, 2014.

## Plot

At a toga party thrown by Magnus, Tobin drunkenly promises to make Gus a board-member--a decision he regrets the next morning. After being unassigned at Omnicore, Wally Pomp finds others like him, who have made careers out of doing nothing. Tobin struggles to put Fernwright's vision into words for a presentation without Gus; later, he discovers an interesting connection between Magnus and Aldous Vane.

## Synopsis

Tobin goes to meet Eli Cross, Magnus Quill’s lawyer. The meeting freaks Tobin out, as Eli reveals that Magnus is funding several other compression start-ups. When Tobin tries to explain his company, Eli loses interest.

At Omnicore, the codelads work away on Helix and consult Wally Pomp for help understanding Tobin’s code so they can steal it, but of course Wally Pomp is no help. A rep from human resources then tells Wally Pomp he’s moved off the Helix project -- but also, that he’s not being fired. Wally Pomp finds a whole world of guys who are “unassigned” at Omnicore -- there’s a group of them who hang out on the roof all day and drink beer. They’re waiting out their contracts, doing what they call “rest and vest.”

Tobin, Arjun, Vex, and Gus go to a toga party thrown by Magnus Quill. There, Nia tells Tobin that Fernwright is officially launched. Tobin is still really grappling with his vision for what the company is -- a fact that becomes even more apparent during his awkward interaction with Nia at the party. Flo Rida performs, and Gus gets on stage with him. Tobin gets really drunk and wakes up the next morning to find that he changed Fernwright’s board to include Gus, who couldn’t be more excited. Wynton tells him he doesn’t have to make the change, and Gus loses it when Tobin says that Gus isn’t on the board.

Tobin and Wynton go to meet with Magnus Quill once again to explain what the company is, but Tobin freaks out beforehand in the bathroom. Wynton helps him get through it -- and even switches pants with him -- but ultimately Tobin goes to the meeting with Gus at his side. Gus really wows Magnus Quill, and Tobin adds Gus back to Fernwright’s board.
