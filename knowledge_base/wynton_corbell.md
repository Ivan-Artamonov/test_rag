# Wynton Corbell

Wallace "Wynton" Corbell is one of the main characters of Beacon's Circadia Valley who works as part of the Fernwright team. Although his real first name is Wallace, Aldous Vane started calling him 'Wynton' on his first day at Omnicore, and the name stuck.

## Character History

### Early Life

Like with most of the main character of the series, we don't know much about Wynton's history before the start of the series, though it is implied to be dark. In Season three Wynton tells Gus that he doesn't know his date of birth as his first CPS worker never located his birth certificate. In season Six Wynton tracks down his parents and finds that they had children before and after him and that they give him up because they wanted to simplify their first-class air travel. It's implied that Wynton was placed in the American adoption system where he wasn't treated well and which leads him to yell in German whilst asleep. His best friends were Priya, a stuffed animal made out of a Ziploc bag, old newspaper, and marker, and an imaginary Harriet Tubman. "We were always planning our big escape," he has said of the latter.

At some point Wynton must have been adopted as he refers to an Aunt and Uncle Jerry and the mean comments they make about him such as his Uncle saying that he has "Ghost like features" and that he "looks like somebody tried to starve a virgin to death". His Aunt also refer to him as glasshole due to his the fragility of his posterior. Despite this rocky early life he went on to study at Vassar College, earning a B. A. in Economics and is working at Omnicore

### Show History

At the start of the first season of the show, Wynton is working at Omnicore as an exective member of staff. He witnesses the programming Bros looking at the Fernwright music app who realise that the algorithm is revolutionary and runs off to inform Aldous Vane what they have discovered. He is seen to have been a member of staff who worked closely with Aldous and is seen around him a lot during the first episode. After Tobin turns down Aldous's offer of $6 Million to build the company himself, Wynton quits Omnicore and begs to join Fernwright which he sees as having a huge potential. He is hired by Tobin for his Business expertise and helps Tobin create business plans and the registering of the business.

In "Third Party Insourcing" he takes a meeting with Nia Calloway to get some Business documents signed and is offered the use of Magnus Quill’s driverless car to get home. He gives the address and everything is going well until the car is overridden and with the address redirected to the new island Magnus is build in the middle of the sea. He is unable to stop this or get out of the car and ends up traveling 1,000 of miles over seas to the Island which is occupied only by the series of Robots. Whilst not stated how he got back, it's shown that he was only able to get back just a day before the start of the next episode.

In "Server Space", it is revealed Wynton has been sleeping in the Quarry's garage having converted it into a bedroom so he could take less of a salary, saving the company money. Once his belongings are discovered, it is decided that he'll room with Tobin, as the others are unwilling to, creating an uncomfortable sleeping arrangement for Tobin due to his unconscious conversations in German. At the end of the episode, after essentially blackmailing a mutually assured destruction of their neighbor, Ezra, it is agreed that Wynton will stay in Ezra's guest house for an "exceedingly reasonable" amount of rent, utilities included.

We find out in "Two in the Cube" that Wynton's looking forward to returning to his condo and moving out of Ezra's guest house due to Ezra using a lot of hate speech towards him. When he returns to his condo, he realizes that his AirBnB tenant has decided to squat in his home after Wynton asks him to leave. After Wynton finds out that the eviction process can take up to a year, Arjun asks him where he will stay in the mean time, to which Wynton replies "I always have my trick: I simply imagine my skeleton is me, and my body is my house and that way I'm always home." Afterwards we find out he's occupying a corner of the garage again.

## Personality

Wynton has a universally friendly demeanor, even when he's insulted to his face, which makes him less respected with the developers. He is well-versed in everything a business requires to operate and assists Tobin in matters of business, but as well as on morale and integrity, while being completely devoted to him. However, his devotion is not necessarily tied to Tobin, as the implications of Tobins conduct led Wynton to assist and care for Guart.

It is shown that he follows the Circadia Valley's culture of hiring gender-equally, displaying all the HR antics of "encouraging" women in tech, eventually leading Zara to consider a lawsuit about sexual harassment against Fernwright for Wynton's behavior.

It is also shown in the series that his demeanor veils a much darker side of his. Being a product of forced adoption and foster care, he learned to dam up his negative tendencies, anger and sadism. With no vent to express them, he leaves it to his subconscious to find balance, making him dream he would be an Austrian criminal who holds women hostage. He also psychologically abuses Pierce as part of Pierce's training to become his successor at Fernwright, to the reserved praise of Arjun and Tobin. He even turned against Tobin when the latter brought up his darker side by provoking him by demeaning Guart.

Eventually it became clear that assistants like him are comparable to caretakers, which naturally became his job after leaving the Circadia Valley.

## Hobbies and interests

In college, Wynton sang second alto with the "Joyce Carol Notes A Capella Confrontation". He participated in competitive rowing as the coxswain of the Women's Heavyweight Crew. He was also the writer and assistant stage manager of the opera "American HerStory XX"

In "Bad Money", Wynton makes several analogies using examples from several Julia Roberts movies, showing he's a fan of her films. However, Wynton's never seen My Best Friend's Wedding.

He makes reference to being into bird watching but its implied that the activity has gone wrong for him in the past when he says "It's like when somebody says they want to go birding with you, but really they just want to get you alone in the woods so they can take your binoculars". His interest in bird watching actually saves the company at one point due to suggesting that they convert a livestream they had made for the energy drink company Vitalize after they betrayed Fernwright into a livestream of a condor nest, which while his suggestion was mocked by the team at first, becomes vital for their leg-up against Helix and Cubix after an animal rescue worker becomes injured and trapped, raking in millions of views from people entertained by the sight.

## Relationships

Despite the dark and traumatic childhood he went through, he is easily the most together and socially and financially stable member of Fernwright. He says that he voluntarily stopped dating in order to give Fernwright his full attention which proves to be true as he is shown to easily get dates with women who enjoy their time with him and are easily willing to spend the night in his garage bedroom.

Out of all of the Fernwright team, Wynton gets on best with Tobin Mercer, who he acts as his righthand man during Fernwright related business matters. He is shown to have become a very dedicated with Tobin and Fernwright and is often personally hurt when Tobin lies or does something dishonest. When Tobin, Vex and Arjun get caught setting up Wifi Pineapples at Omnicore-Con in order to get people on their network, Wynton is angry that Tobin risked Fernwrights future after a immature prank on a fellow convention member and yells at the rest of the team.

He is frequently roasted by the other employees of Fernwright though he tends to be oblivious of this or too uptight to actually understand the insult. Wynton is usually depicted as the most empathetic person on the team, given most of the team's moral ambiguity. At the start of the series Vex and Arjun are openly hostile to Wynton and treat him as an outsider but admit that he is good at the business side of things. They are resistant to many of his business schemes such as the SWOT system, SCRUM, or his attempts to get them to work faster.

In "Artificial Emotional Intelligence" Wynton hires Pierce to become Tobin's new secretary, but as the course of the series goes on their relationship grows increasingly from your average training experience to one that even other members of the main three Fernwright team begin questioning how intensely devastating Wynton's influence over Pierce had become with side remarks about "Wynton really did a number on him," during a particularly zealous show of excitement from Pierce after the company had begun to succeed again. Wynton's training method's are never revealed, but we can see some of how terrified Pierce is of Wynton poking through in scenes where they're featured together throughout the season.

In "Artificial Lack of Intelligence", after feeling like there's little else he can do for Tobin and the company, he heads back to the Quarry to regain some sense of himself and finds Wally Pomp has created a new incubator with Hwan-Soo Park. After having a "walk down memory-lane", he meets Nyssa, an anti-social mute incubee (who actually has a cameo lasting less than a minute in the first season's episode "Fiduciary Duties" at the toga party), and immediately transfers his caretaker role from Tobin to Nyssa. Wynton is fiercely defensive of Nyssa and assumes her every need with only looks from her as confirmation or disappointment. In the episode "Blood Money", Wynton becomes enraged at Tobin after he insults Nyssa, showing a side of Wynton before unseen where he tells Tobin to "keep her name out of your skanky little mouth, you little, little bitch!" Wynton then shoots at Tobin with a BB-gun yelling at Tobin while he flees "Dope-led, peckerwood motherfucker!" After kicking the bathroom door Tobin has locked himself into, he asks him "you wanna dance on the blacktop, homie!?" And continues to shoot at Tobin from the front lawn. In the episode "Omnicore Smokes!" Wynton finds out Tobin told Aldous Vane about Nyssa's "sexy" tech, causing Hwan-Soo Park to sell Nyssa's company to Aldous for pocket change. Wynton angrily calls Tobin to confront him about it, thinking that Tobin's actions were out of spite he tells him that if he wanted revenge against Wynton, that was fine with him, but to go after Nyssa was unforgiveable. He comes into the office later in semi-disguise to give Fernwright information about Omnicore revealing that the company has been run into the ground by Aldous, meaning Fernwright could buy Omnicore and thereby releasing Nyssa and Wynton to become independent. By the end of the episode, Tobin apologizes to Wynton, repairing their relationship and offering a workspace for Nyssa in his office. In "Maximizing Alphaness" after Nyssa meets with Heloise Voss, the two take a liking to one another and Nyssa decides to dissolve her company to seek a job elsewhere, and fires Wynton from her company. Wynton immediately returns to work for Tobin, getting rid of Pierce as his previous secretary.
