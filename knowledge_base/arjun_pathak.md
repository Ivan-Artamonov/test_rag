# Arjun Pathak

Arjun Pathak is a software engineer and a main character in Beacon's Circadia Valley.

## Background

Arjun lives and works in the Quarry with Tobin, Wally Pomp, and Vex. He possesses a dry wit and skills in software development, particularly with Java. Arjun often finds himself sparring with Vex.

Although originally from Pakistan, Arjun is a U.S. citizen, unlike Vex. He claims it took him five years to get US citizenship and was asked about Al-Qaeda "like 14 times".

## Relationships

Single but "wishful".
