# Signaling Risk

"You see what he's doing right. He's trying to get us to compete so we work faster. He thinks this wall of Psych 101 MBA mind-control bullshit is going to motivate us."

―Arjun Pathak

Signaling Risk is the fifth episode in season 1 of the Beacon series Circadia Valley. It originally aired on May 4, 2014.

## Plot

Gus hires a convicted felon and graffiti artist, Mateo, to create a new logo for Fernwright and to paint it on the incubator garage, but Mateo's creation depicts Arjun having sexual intercourse with the Statue of Liberty because he believes Arjun is of Latin descent.

Fernwright gains entry to the DevPulse Startup Battlefield, a technology conference that pits unseeded startup companies against each other, with $50,000 as the grand prize - the only problem is, Fernwright is not unseeded, and this angers Magnus. Tobin suggests withdrawing from the Battlefield, but Vane beats him to it and announces the release of Helix will take place at the Battlefield, and due to Magnus's intense rivalry with Vane, he refuses to allow Fernwright to withdraw and tells them to have a finished demo for the Battlefield in eight weeks.

## Synopsis

Gus takes Arjun and Vex on a quest to find graffiti artist Mateo Cruz and convince him to create the Fernwright logo. Gus wants Mateo to give Fernwright a cool vibe, but the artist demands stock options because a friend of his got them when he created murals for Facebook. While Gus negotiates with Mateo in an alley, Arjun and Vex wait with the car. Mateo spots Arjun and says that he thinks it’s cool that Fernwright hired a Latino. Gus doesn’t correct the artist about Arjun’s true ethnicity because that seems to be the thing that made Mateo agree to work on a logo.

Gus promised Mateo $10,000 for the logo, but Wynton points out that Fernwright is going to run out of money long before the product is ready, even without spending on the logo. Wynton further freaks out when he learns that Fernwright has been accepted to DevPulse Expo Startup Alley, a move that doesn’t make sense for the company since it’s already been funded. Tobin explains to Wynton that he put the application in long ago and forgot about it.

Wally Pomp is in Aldous Vane’s office for a meeting where the boss’ underling executives are planning to beam him in via holographic technology, which turns out to be rather fraught with glitches. Aldous grills Wally Pomp on why Tobin would be taking Fernwright to DevPulse Expo, but of course, Wally Pomp doesn’t have much info.

Wynton wants Gus and Tobin to define Fernwright’s corporate culture, causing Arjun and Vex to balk at the mention of cubicles. Wynton also announces that he can also do the Fernwright logo himself -- and save the company $10,000. He draws two lower-case Ps on a piece of paper, sending Gus into a rage. Meanwhile, Mateo arrives to work on the logo.

Magnus Quill is having lunch at a restaurant with Nia when Aldous Vane unexpectedly walks through the door. The guys have an awkward conversation where Aldous tells Magnus that he offered to give the keynote speech at DevPulse Expo, which he will also use to unveil Fernwright’s big competition, Helix. Tobin later tells Nia he’s going to pull Fernwright out of DevPulse Expo, and she informs him that he can’t because of Aldous and Magnus’s rivalry. Tobin comes to realize that he’s just a pawn between these two. DevPulse Expo is just two months away, which means that Fernwright has to be ready much sooner than the five months that was originally planned for development.

At the Quarry, Mateo’s Fernwright logo -- which is painted on the Quarry’s garage door -- depicts a Latino Arjun having anal sex with the Statue of Liberty. Gus tries to be gentle in his revision notes, but Mateo is mad and only ends up changing Arjun to be Pakistani and putting Gus’s face on the Statue of Liberty. The police arrive and tells Gus he has to remove the art. In an attempt to fix the problem, Tobin puts up the garage door, which only reveals Gus’s pot. The police say they have to take the weed with them.

Wynton institutes Scrum for the Fernwright guys, in an effort to increase productivity and communication about projects. The tactic instantly works, as Vex and Arjun compete to finish their tasks before the other.

Fernwright settles on a new, lower-cased logo, while it’s revealed that Mateo sold his original Fernwright design for $500,000….to Aldous Vane, who hangs it in the Omnicore offices. Nia arrives at the Quarry to apologize for her part in Tobin’s pawn situation, and she says she believes in the company.
