# Zara Okafor

Zara Okafor (portrayed by Alice Wetterlund) is a engineer that the team hires in "The Lady".

Suggested to be hired by Vex and Arjun Pathak.

Was thought as a person who could have worked on the skunk works.

## Background

An excellent coder and the newest addition to Fernwright, Zara delights herself by keeping the Quarry nerds on their toes.

## Quotes
