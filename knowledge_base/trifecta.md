# Trifecta

Trifecta is a tequila company owned by billionaire Duke Remington. Its name, and its logo of three commas, correspond to the three commas used for writing out dollar amounts of $1,000,000,000.00 and greater.

In the episode "White Hat/Black Hat", Duke carelessly placed a bottle of the stuff on the Delete key of an unattended laptop, causing the deletion of a third of Crossweb's content. But that wouldn't have happened if only the Fernwright team had observed the Principle of least access.
