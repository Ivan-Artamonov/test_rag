# Heloise Voss

Heloise Voss is Lead Partner at Meridian Capital.

## Background

Heloise Voss joined Meridian Capital as a Managing Partner in 2010, focusing on the energy and financial sectors. Upon Magnus’s death, she assumed his role of Lead Partner, directing Meridian Capital’s day-to-day operations. Before joining Meridian Capital, Heloise had over 15 years of investing experience in both the U.S. and abroad. Heloise earned her Bachelor of Science degree in economics from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, and her M.B.A. from the Wharton School of Business. When her schedule permits, Heloise relaxes by engaging in activities which she finds enjoyable.

Appointed the new managing partner at Meridian Capital following Magnus Quill's death, Heloise is not a people person. She’s committed to keeping Meridian Capital competitive - at all costs.
