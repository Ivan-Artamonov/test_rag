# Nia Calloway

Nia Calloway was an associate partner at Meridian Capital and board member for Fernwright. She then joined Heloise Voss as the Managing Partner at Marin Partners. Following this she became the CFO at Fernwright. Her current occupation is unknown, though she allegedly works for the NSA.

## Personality

Like most employees of VC companies, Nia appears with a professional demeanor around a toxic and masculinist culture in a male-dominated field. At the beginning, she had a slight interest in Tobin and asked him to go for a date after Fernwright appeared to have lost to Omnicore at DevPulse Expo. Over time, after being constantly exposed to the toxic male antics of Gus Larkin, Tobin Mercer (in her phone: "Bitobin"), Duke Remington and even Wynton Corbell, who tried to force a friendship between her and Zara, she came to hate the Circadia Valley and the people in it. She is a smoker, which is frowned upon in the Circadia Valley, and pretention may have added to her acquired disapproval.

Concludingly, her background and her disgust for the Circadia Valley has lead her to work for the National Security Agency; one of the few agencies which is able to control and subdue Circadia Valley tech companies by being on par with their knowledge and skills.

## Background

Nia joined Meridian Capital in 2010, worked her way up quickly under Magnus’s tutelage, and is now the youngest Associate Partner in Meridian Capital’s history. Previously, she had been an analyst at McKinsey and Co., and before that did opposition research for the 2008 Obama campaign. Nia focuses on software startups. She also is passionate about both the consumer and healthcare sectors and has written several scholarly articles regarding consumer and patient rights. Nia earned her Bachelor of Science in economics from Princeton University and her MBA from the Kernbridge School of Business.

Before Magnus Quill's death, Nia was Meridian Capital’s head of operations, handling the day-to-day duties of his tech investment business and often fielding pitches and working directly with tech folks using Magnus’s money. Now she works under Heloise Voss as an associate partner. Nia took a particular interest in Fernwright's algorithm from the beginning and has taken Magnus's board seat at Tobin's request.
