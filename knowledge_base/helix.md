# Helix

Helix was an Omnicore project.

## Season 1

Immediately after Tobin declined Aldous's 10 million dollar offer Aldous compiled a group of engineers to reverse engineer Tobin's product. The project was supposed to integrate Tobin's Fernwright compression algorithm into the whole sweep of Omnicore apps. The project was way behind schedule but they successfully engineered a perfect Kraus Index of 2.89.

## Season 2

After being beat by Fernwright's revolutionary fold-in technology at DevPulse Expo they invested into Cubix another fold-in compression company. Using the company's code they started developing a cloud based Compression platform similar to Fernwright's.

## Season 3

After Fernwright's platform failed to succeed Helix was scraped.
