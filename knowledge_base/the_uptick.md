# The Uptick

The Uptick is the tenth and final episode of Season 3, and the twenty-eighth episode overall. It originally aired on June 26, 2016.

## Synopsis

With Fernwright's future in question, Gus's publicity success leaves Tobin in a moral quandary just as Arjun's video-chat app starts to pick up speed. While Heloise prepares to jump ship, Aldous's comeback at Omnicore is threatened by his displays of grandeur.

## Plot

Aldous watches from his office window as the deceased elephant is covered and roped off. Simone is having a tough time disposing of it, and Otis suggests airlifting it into the bay. When Simone voices her concerns about Aldous’s use of endangered animals, she is fired.

Wynton sits Tobin down and prepares to tell him about the fake users. Tobin admits he already knows, and asks Wynton to keep it between them until they run out of money.

Gus proclaims he saved the company by using the uptick to get into the heads of important VCs. Wynton and Tobin sweat when Gus tells them Coleman Blair offered $6 million of Series B funding.

Simone turns in her Omnicore ID. On the guard’s computer screen, she sees Blythe’s blog soliciting tech dirt, and an idea strikes her.
Vex and Arjun corner Tobin about the fake users. They ask him if he has seen Arjun’s “lost” flash drive that possesses a zombie script that randomizes user actions, so that VCs could not detect clickfarm behavior. Arjun tells Tobin to “keep an eye out for it” as he pulls the drive from his pocket and places it on the ground.

Tobin brings Wynton the flash drive, to buy them time until the platform catches on. Wynton is not convinced -- “Don’t weaponize my faith in you against me,” -- but Tobin does not want to do the right thing.

Aldous’s “Box” consultation with Keung is interrupted by a call from Blythe asking for a statement regarding “the death of an unpermitted Indian elephant named Maurice … and its subsequent illegal dumping in the San Francisco Bay.” Aldous asks what he can do to prevent this story coming out, and ends up buying Blythe’s blog for $2 million.

Waiting for the meeting at Coleman Blair, Tobin gets a call from Nia who is furious that they’re raising money somewhere else. Tobin prepares to sign the term sheet, but when it is in front of him, he panics, and pitches the video chat platform. Coleman Blair ups the offer thinking Tobin’s hesitation is a negotiating ploy, so Tobin reveals the uptick was fake. Gus storms out.

Nia goes to the Quarry and is greeted by Arjun and Vex who “had no idea any of that stuff was going on.” Nia goes to Tobin’s room and Arjun gets a glitchy video chat from Gleb. When he goes to fix it, he notices the server is overloaded.

Nia tells Tobin and Wynton that Heloise is forcing a sale because “even a whiff of fraud is a mortal sin for VCs.”

Arjun and Vex realize the video chat app is rapidly spreading. Gus announces that he is going to India and he expects Tobin “off the premises by end of the day.”

Aldous and Declan run into Heloise, who asks Aldous if he is interested in buying Fernwright. Aldous, eager “to drive the final nail into the Mercer coffin,” offers $900 cash. Heloise rejects his joke, and Aldous ups the ante to $1 million.

Tobin video chats with Nia who tells him about the pending sale. Wally Pomp notices the chat is much better than Omnicore Chat as he prepares to go back to Phoenix with his father.

Fernwright has a board meeting to vote on the sale to Omnicore. Meridian Capital, which has board majority, prepares to approve the sale, but Nia refuses and is fired. Heloise calls in a random employee to vote yes, but Evan refuses because he is in love with Nia. Tobin votes yes to get the sale over with, and to everyone’s surprise, Fernwright is sold to Larkinity LLC -- the product of a last-minute bid.

Tobin finds Gus sitting by the pool and thanks him for saving the company. The gang is back at it in the Quarry for another game of “always green” and they discuss redoing the company’s apportionment of stock.
