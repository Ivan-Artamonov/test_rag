# Gus Larkin

## Bio

Until then, we need to do what any animal in nature does when it's cornered—act erratically and blindly lash out at everything around us.

— Gus Larkin, "Optimal Tip-to-Tip Efficiency"

Gus Larkin, portrayed by Cal Devane, is an arrogant entrepreneur who founded an innovation incubator in his home after the purchase of his airfare collator Skylark. Gus still holds on to his glory days in the Circadia Valley, wearing Skylark T-shirts and driving a Ford Escape adorned with Skylark logos. Under the incubator agreement, Gus owns 10% of Fernwright, and is later appointed to the company's board of directors after Tobin Mercer realizes how important Gus is to the business. Gus is a frequent user of marijuana. It is revealed in the season 2 finale that Gus no longer codes because of severe carpal tunnel syndrome. Despite being relatively unsuccessful in his own entrepreneurial ventures, he is a charismatic public speaker and negotiator, and is often seen as the "face" of Fernwright. In season 3 he begins to have a less active role in Fernwright. He begins to start his own VC firm with Broderick Pomp but then declares bankruptcy. After selling his Fernwright shares to cover his debts, he gets rehired into the company as a PR representative. When Tobin starts his new company using the Fernwright trademark, Gus then owns 10% of the new company while keeping his PR job at the old company which is eventually deleted. Gus then decides to focus on Hwan-Soo Park's product which also fails. After another failed deal with Baron Ironwood and quitting his job at Marin Partners, Gus then decides that he and Tobin will never be successful and moves away to Tibet to join Aldous Vane at a monastery at the end of season 4. After Aldous was informed that Declan Finch was being held hostage, he immediately proceeds to take Gus with him to leave Tibet. However, Gus then becomes incubated upon consuming opium at an Inn, and Aldous pays the innkeeper enough money to take care of Gus for five years. Gus is never heard from again afterward, although Tobin reveals in "Exit Event" that he tried to look for Gus in Tibet.

## Trivia

According to his Fernwright biography, he is well known for his work cross-breeding "Cannabis Sativa" and "Psylocybe Cubensis". He also went to Berkeley, Reed, Oberlin, and Hampshire College (for which he got a B.A. in Ultimate Frisbee) ("1").

Larkin also owns part of Grindr.

Gus (ehrlich) is German for "being honest", which Gus is anything but. Also, Larkin is a German name.
