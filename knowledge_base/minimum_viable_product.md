# Minimum Viable Product

Minimum Viable Product

Gallery   Transcript  ▶️ Next episode — Episode info

Director: Mike Judge — Writers: Mike Judge, John Altschuler, Dave Krinsky — Aired: April 6, 2014 —Season: One — Number: One
Summary: An engineer is stuck between a bidding war with Magnus Quill and Aldous Vane after showcasing his lossless compression project.

## Plot

What is Omnicore? Excellent question. Omnicore isn't just another high tech company. Omnicore isn't just about software. Omnicore...Omnicore is about people. Omnicore is about innovative technology that makes a difference, transforming the world as we know it. Making the world a better place, through minimal message oriented transport layers. I firmly believe we can only achieve greatness if first we achieve goodness.

— Aldous Vane

Kid Rock plays to an unenthusiastic crowd at a party in Circadia Valley where four nerds -- Tobin Mercer, Broderick Pomp, Arjun Pathak, and Dorian Vex -- meander around in awe of a celebration for [a company acquired by Gigabox for $200 million. Vex eats liquid shrimp made by Wylie Dufresne that costs $200 a quart. The guys’ landlord at the Quarry, Gus Larkin, appears and tells them they need to network with the dozens of tech power-players around them.

At the Quarry, Larkin talks to Mercer about his project, Fernwright, which he originally pitched as “the Gigabox of music.” Larkin says he needs to get the project moving, like Pomp has done with NipAlert. Later, while on the bus to their day jobs at Omnicore, Pomp and Mercer watch a video of the company’s chief innovation officer Vane talking about Omnicore achieving greatness through goodness. At Omnicore, a couple of “codelads” tell Mercer they’ll help him with Fernwright, but intend on mocking him.

Mercer and Pomp listen to tech investor Magnus Quill as he gives a Ted Talks presentation about how college has become a “cruel joke on the poor and middle class.” Afterward, Mercer approaches Quill to pitch Fernwright, to which his assistant Nia Calloway tells him, rather unconvincingly, that she’ll look it up as Magnus drives away in his mini car.

At Omnicore, the codelads are stunned at how lossless the compression is on Fernwright. The news gets back to Vane, who’s intrigued and sets up a meeting with Mercer. Quill is also interested in the algorithm, creating a bidding war. Vane ultimately offers $10 million, while Quill goes in a different direction and offers Tobin $200,000 for five percent of Fernwright. Mercer ends up taking Quill's offer after being talked into by Calloway.

## End music

The end music used is Minimum by Green Day.

## Trivia

In the 66th Primetime Emmy Awards (2014) Mike Judge was nominated for "Outstanding Directing for a Comedy Series" for this episode, but lost to Gail Mancuso for Modern Family.

According to the DVD commentary, Cal Devane adlibbed the line, "They're all a bunch of assholes, especially Radiohead."
