# Declan Finch

Declan "Action Declan" Finch was a main character in seasons 3 & 4 in Beacon's Circadia Valley.

Declan is first mentioned by Nia in the episode Founder Friendly as being the new replacement CEO of Fernwright after Tobin was voted out of by the board members of Meridian Capital. He then appears later in the same episode when Gus Larkin goes to meet him at the Meridian Capital offices in an attempt to intimidate him with jokes based towards his age.

Tobin then goes to visit Declan at his home to inform him that Tobin intends to turn down the position of CTO at Fernwright. Due to this Declan no longer has an interest in being the CEO because of Tobins work on the algorithm, however Tobin then reconsiders his position and agrees to stay as CTO leading Declan to accept the position of CEO.
