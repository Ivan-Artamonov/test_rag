# Larkinity Insanity

Larkinity Insanity is the sixth episode in Season 3 of the Beacon series Circadia Valley, and the twenty-fourth episode overall. It originally aired on May 29, 2016.

## Synopsis

Tobin's neuroses could ruin his new relationship. Wally Pomp and Gus's launch party goes awry. Arjun is smitten with a foreign coworker.

## Plot

The guys celebrate Tobin's re-instatement as CEO of Fernwright at a local Mexican restaurant. Over margaritas, Tobin reveals he got the number of a good-looking woman at the bar. With the exception of Wynton, the guys can't believe a woman would be interested in Tobin and insist she's either a man, the bartender being nice, or a "founder hounder."

Back at the Quarry, Tobin video chats with his engineers, calling one of them out for using spaces, not tabs, in his code. Arjun starts a separate chat afterwards with pretty engineer Elisabet but Vex is skeptical of the genuineness of her flirtation.

Blythe Aldridge’s “Aldous Vane’s Omnicore-Scrub Scandal” goes live on Byte/Chirp, and protesters come out in force outside Omnicore. Aldous threatens to sue Aldridge unless she reveals her source; she alerts Wally Pomp about Aldous’s threat. Gus promises to deal with the situation after Wally Pomp tells him there’s a chance he'll lose his settlement money for violating his non-disclosure agreement.

Arjun continues to flirt with Elisabet via video chat, though Vex reminds him the quality is poor and she could actually be ugly. The guys learn that Priya, the woman Tobin met at the restaurant, spent the night. While Tobin gets his keys to drive her home, Priya makes small talk with Vex and Arjun. When they find out she codes with spaces, not tabs, they warn her against revealing the information to Tobin.

Wally Pomp meets with his business manager, Arthur, who worries Wally Pomp is allowing Gus to take advantage of him. Just then, Gus calls to tell Wally Pomp he solved the Aldous problem and made a "strategic investment" at the same time: He bought Byte/Chirp from Aldridge for half a million dollars.

Motivated by his developing flirtation with Elisabet, Arjun figures out a way to use the Fernwright compression for RTC, creating a better resolution for video chatting.

At the Quarry, Gus hands out invitations to "Larkinity Insanity" -- the luau-themed party he (and ostensibly Wally Pomp) are hosting at Alcatraz to launch their partnership.

Later, at Priya’s apartment, Tobin and Priya get into an argument after she reveals her preference for spaces over tabs, and Tobin is unable to let it go. Tobin breaks up with her and leaves the apartment, only to fall down the stairs on his way out.

When Elisabet sees what Arjun looks like through his new high resolution video chat, she claims she has a husband and quickly signs off.

Arjun and Tobin glumly sip drinks at the "Larkinity" launch party. Gus tells them he's about to take the stage, where he plans to rub his latest success in the faces of his "fiercest rivals." His party planner, Sasha, pulls him aside to tell him several vendors claim their checks bounced. Gus rushes to speak with Wally Pomp, who's sitting with Arthur. Arthur reveals that with the purchase of Byte/Chirp and the lavish party, along with other irresponsible spending, Gus and Wally Pomp are functionally insolvent.

## Notes

The tabs vs. spaces debate is a real thing, but in real life it had already been pretty much resolved, so that some of what happens in this episode would be unnecessary in real life.
