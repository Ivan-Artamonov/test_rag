# Sagar

## Bio

You clearly have a great understanding of humanity.

— Sagar Singh, "Minimum Viable Product".

File:Sagar head shot.jpg

Sagar Singh portrayed by Bernard White.

Sagar Singh was Aldous Vane's spiritual advisor during his time at Omnicore, but was fired when Aldous Vane left Omnicore to work on his novels. His first appearance was in the Season One episode "Minimum Viable Product". He is portrayed by actor Bernard White.
