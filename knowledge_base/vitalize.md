# Vitalize

Vitalize is the sixth episode in Season 2 of the Beacon series Circadia Valley.

## Synopsis

When Nia encourages the guys to pounce on a livestream opportunity, Gus reconnects with his old mentee, Big-Z, an energy drink billionaire, but Tobin finds out their friendship isn’t quite what Gus thinks. After learning that the livestream might hit a snag, Vex and Arjun brainstorm their options. Wynton encourages a friendship between Zara and Nia.

## Plot

Omnicore and Fernwright employees gather, separately, to watch the Omnicore UFC livestream. When Nia arrives at the Quarry, Wynton introduces her to Zara, in a very awkward and unsolicited effort to make the women friends. "I knew you two would hit it off," Wynton says. As the stream begins, the Fernwright team notices glitches in the stream. Aldous does, as well, but the executives and programmers around him act like nothing is amiss. Eventually the stream freezes. "F**!" Aldous screams as he storms out of the screening.

Nia sees an opportunity in Omnicore's failure, suggesting Fernwright do its own livestream. Tobin is apprehensive. Wynton suggests streaming the hatching of a condor egg, but Gus says he'll reach out to his friend, Bruno "Big-Z" Andersen, who owns Vitalize Energy Drink, to see about streaming one of their extreme events. Tobin still isn't sure about it, so Wynton suggests using SWOT -- a decision-making matrix that stands for strengths, weaknesses, opportunities and threats -- to figure out if they should partner with Vitalize.

Big-Z explains how the extreme jumping event will work, detailing the 27 cameras that will feed into Tobin's livestream. Gus keeps interrupting to say how "awesome" everything is. About Gus, Big-Z tells Tobin, "To be honest, I could never stand the guy. Just hearing his voice again is giving me PTSD."

Arjun and Vex meet Decker, the guy who's doing the Vitalize stunt. He's wholly uninterested in meeting the pair, who realize that the stuntman calculated the velocity for his stunt incorrectly and will die if he doesn't change it. Decker is rude when Vex tries to point out the blunder. "What do we do here?" Arjun asks. Vex responds: "This is a tough one." Big-Z tells Tobin to lose Gus. "I love your tech," he says, "but come back tomorrow without Kool-Aid or don't come back at all. Honestly."

Sagar counsels Aldous about the Helix situation., asking, "Have I just surrounded myself with sycophants who are just telling me whatever I want to hear regardless of the truth?" Sagar swallows deeply and assures him, "No." Aldous likes this response: "Thank you, Sagar. I really needed to hear that." Later, Aldous watches a focus group talking about the Helix operating system on an Omnicore phone. "It's just stupid," one guy says. "Well, it's slow and the apps keep quitting on me."

Arjun and Vex continue to debate the Decker conundrum. "This might be morbid, but the cold, hard fact is that if Decker died on our livestream, this could be good for us," Vex says. "We'd get a lot more traffic." Arjun questions the moral issues around not saying anything, deciding to apply SWOT to the situation. Tobin explains to Gus that Big-Z doesn't want him around. Gus says he'll step aside but warns Tobin that Big-Z isn't to be trusted -- people used to call him "Big-Jerk," Gus says.

While Arjun and Vex SWOT the Decker decision, the stuntman walks into the room and apologies for being rude the day before and mentions spotting an error in his math that would have probably killed him. "Are we cool?" he asks the guys. Just before leaving, Decker sees the SWOT board and is enraged. "Would you believe," Arjun says of the board, "that was here when we came in?"

Tobin confronts Big-Z about not using the Fernwright logo on the video frame but he refuses to change it. Tobin loses it. "You know what Big-Z?" he says. "Gus was right. You are an asshole. In fact, you're a Big-Jerk." Big-Z pulls up his shirt to reveal a colostomy bag. "Do you have any idea what it's like to be 10 years old and carry your own shit around in a bag?" he says. "To have two assholes?" Arjun and Vex scurry past and grab Tobin on the way out.

At the Quarry, Gus finds the situation hilarious. "You tore Big-Jerk a third asshole!" he says, laughing uncontrollably. Wynton convinces the guys to use their livestream to instead show the hatching of the condor egg. Duke Remington calls to congratulate Tobin on the livestream -- and new name for the company, Cubix, which is the company credited for the Vitalize event. "Wait, Cubix isn't you guys?" Duke asks. Arjun figures out what's going on: "Cubix is those guys who brain-raped us," he realizes. Tobin is again enraged: "Those pricks! They stole my algorithm." Tobin explains to Duke how Cubix got their technology, prompting Duke to hang up the phone.
